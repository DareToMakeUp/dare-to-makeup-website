About Anghela

A story built one detail at a time

It all began in 2018 — with the basics, a passion for enhancing every person's natural beauty, and a strong drive to learn. Anghela started step by step, perfecting each technique with dedication, becoming a licensed cosmetologist and specializing in lash extensions and micropigmentation.

In 2024, she took an important step: she arrived in the United States to keep building her career, and that's how Dare to MakeUp was born — a space where elegance, technique and attention to detail come together in every service.

Today, with over 7 years of experience, Anghela carries the same philosophy she started with: every face is unique, and deserves to be treated with precision, care and excellence.
